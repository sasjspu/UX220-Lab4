Comparison of Old-Fashioned Typesetting and Desktop Publishing

Old-fashioned typesetting is the process of producing physical text publications by arranging physical letters in a typesetting machine (such as a Linotype machine), inking them, and then printing them.

Desktop publishing, on the other hand, uses HTML to create and structure webpages on the internet and CSS to style the webpages. It is much quicker and easier than old-fashioned typesetting.
